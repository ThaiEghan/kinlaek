Meet Our Owner & Head Chef

25+ Years Exp.

Jenny & Chef Prachya Priyanu

Founder & Master Chef of KIN LAEK (กินแหลก)

KIN LAEK is founded by Jenny (Jariya Priyanu), together with her husband, master Head Chef Prachya Priyanu. With a shared culinary passion spanning over 25 years in the UK, they bring time-honored Thai traditions and heartfelt hospitality to every plate.

Their extraordinary journey began in Colnbrook, expanded through Staines, and now proudly thrives in Egham. Bringing decades of experience to the community, they offer authentic, handcrafted Thai food made with love and dedication.
