Jariya Priyanu (Chef Jenny)

Founder & Master Chef of Kin laek

With over 25 years of extraordinary culinary expertise in the United Kingdom, Chef Jenny (Jariya Priyanu) is a celebrated master of authentic Thai gastronomy. Every dish at Kin laek is personally handcrafted by Chef Jenny using time-honored traditional recipes.

Chef Jenny established her outstanding reputation as the former owner and head chef of the renowned Bong Thai at The Punch Bowl Pub. Bringing decades of passion to Egham, she acquired EAT THAI and transformed it into Kin laek — offering a culinary haven for lovers of true Thai flavors.
